# Photo scorecard

For Antonio. Adopted October 24, 2026.

Use this card to grade any candidate photograph as KEEP, EDIT, or RETAKE. It applies the [photo rulebook](photo-rulebook.md); each line links to the rule that explains it.

Grade the unedited original, at full size, without the site's filter. Then look at it once at card size, about 8 cm (3 in) wide.

## Step 1: gates

Every gate must pass. One failure means RETAKE, whatever the scores.

| Gate                       | Passes when                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | Rule                                                       |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| G1 Ours                    | The family took it, and Antonio confirms it as an original.                                                                                                                                                                                | [Plan](recipe-photography.md#missing-photographs)          |
| G2 Right dish, right state | It shows this recipe. A cover shows the finished dish; a process photo shows the step's cue. Nothing on the food is missing from the recipe. A side dish, if present, covers less than a quarter of the frame and sits outside the centre. | [9](photo-rulebook.md#9-styling)                           |
| G3 Sharp where it matters  | At 100 % zoom, the front edge of the main food is crisp. No motion blur.                                                                                                                                                                   | [6.2](photo-rulebook.md#62-focus-on-the-front-of-the-food) |
| G4 Survives the crops      | A 4:3 landscape crop exists whose middle band, the centre 64 % of its height, still shows what makes the dish recognisable.                                                                                                                | [1](photo-rulebook.md#1-frame-for-the-site)                |
| G5 Private                 | No faces, no children or children's hands, no screens, mail, documents, addresses, or identifying window views.                                                                                                                            | [11](photo-rulebook.md#11-privacy)                         |
| G6 Big enough              | That 4:3 crop is at least 1600 px wide. Photos saved from messaging apps often fail this.                                                                                                                                                  | [Plan](recipe-photography.md#image-preparation)            |

## Step 2: scores

Score each criterion 0, 1, or 2. Use the descriptions; do not average between them. A ✂ marks a criterion that an edit can raise by one point.

| Code | Criterion            | 2                                                                                                                  | 1                                                                              | 0                                                                                         | Rule                                                                                 |
| ---- | -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| L    | Light                | One soft light from the side or behind. Soft shadows show texture.                                                 | Flat room light from above, or soft light from the front.                      | Flash, hard direct sun, mixed light colours, or the photographer's shadow on the food.    | [2](photo-rulebook.md#2-light)                                                       |
| E ✂  | Exposure             | No clipped areas on the food. Shadows hold detail.                                                                 | Somewhat dark or bright, but detail is all there.                              | Blown white areas on the food, or murky and noisy.                                        | [3](photo-rulebook.md#3-exposure)                                                    |
| C ✂  | Colour               | Whites are neutral. Food looks as it did on the table.                                                             | A mild single cast, such as slightly yellow or blue.                           | Two casts at once, or a heavy filter or saturation baked in.                              | [4](photo-rulebook.md#4-colour)                                                      |
| A    | Angle                | Matches the dish's angle and shows its defining feature.                                                           | Between angles, often the standing-at-the-counter 60°. Feature partly visible. | Hides the defining feature, such as overhead on a stack.                                  | [5](photo-rulebook.md#5-angle)                                                       |
| Co ✂ | Composition          | The dish fills half to four fifths of the 4:3 frame and reads at card size. Edges are clean or decisively cropped. | Subject small, or slivers of plate clipped at the edges.                       | Subject unclear at card size.                                                             | [7](photo-rulebook.md#7-composition)                                                 |
| B    | Background and props | Calm, matte, from the reference set or the recipe's own vessel.                                                    | One distraction that a crop removes.                                           | Busy pattern, packaging, clutter, or a bright unrelated object that a crop cannot remove. | [8](photo-rulebook.md#8-background-and-props)                                        |
| S    | Styling              | Fresh, clean rims, real portion, appetising.                                                                       | Small smears, slightly dry, or served in a storage or transport container.     | Looks unappetising: congealed, dried out, or messy.                                       | [9](photo-rulebook.md#9-styling)                                                     |
| T    | Texture              | The defining texture is clear: crust, crumb, grains, pegao, gloss.                                                 | Visible only at full size.                                                     | Not visible.                                                                              | [2.1](photo-rulebook.md#21-use-one-soft-light-from-the-side)                         |
| R    | Fits the set         | Would sit naturally beside the reference covers in the grid.                                                       | Noticeably different in light, warmth, or surface, but not jarring.            | Would clash.                                                                              | [8](photo-rulebook.md#8-background-and-props)                                        |
| G ✂  | Geometry and edit    | Level, natural proportions, light-handed edit.                                                                     | Slight tilt or keystone.                                                       | 0.5× distortion, Portrait-mode blur errors, HDR halos, or heavy filter.                   | [6](photo-rulebook.md#6-lens-focus-and-geometry), [10](photo-rulebook.md#10-editing) |

Until the reference set exists, score R against the direction in the rulebook: soft side light, neutral whites, matte neutral surfaces.

## Step 3: verdict

| Verdict | When                                                                                                                            |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| KEEP    | All gates pass, the total is 16 or more out of 20, and no criterion scores 0.                                                   |
| EDIT    | All gates pass, and raising each ✂ criterion by at most one point would reach KEEP. Edit, then score again. It must reach KEEP. |
| RETAKE  | Any gate fails, or neither rule above applies.                                                                                  |

A RETAKE that passes every gate may be published as an interim cover until the retake exists, if Antonio prefers a real photo to the placeholder. Mark it as interim in the [completion tracker](recipe-photography.md#completion-tracker).

For a RETAKE, write the two lowest scores as the brief for the next session. That is the lesson to practise.

## Recording a grade

Use one row per candidate. Keep the notes short and specific enough to act on.

```
| Dish | Candidate | Gates | L | E | C | A | Co | B | S | T | R | G | Total | Verdict | Fix or retake brief |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Arroz con Pollo | IMG_1234 | pass | 2 | 1 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 19 | KEEP | Lower highlights slightly on the rice when exporting. |
```

Write a failed gate as its code, for example "G4 fail". Use the file name or library identifier for the candidate, never a local path.

## Worked examples

These grade the photographs live on the site on October 24, 2026. Look at each photo while you read its grade; the reasoning is the lesson.

### Fried Chicken Tender Nuggets cover

Gates pass. For G4, the photo is portrait, but a 4:3 crop through the tenders keeps them in the middle band.

| L   | E   | C   | A   | Co  | B   | S   | T   | R   | G   | Total | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | ----- | ------- |
| 1   | 2   | 1   | 1   | 1   | 0   | 2   | 2   | 1   | 2   | 13    | RETAKE  |

- **L 1.** Room light from above. The breading shows texture because it is rough, not because the light reveals it.
- **C 1.** Warm and slightly orange. Under the site filter the chicken turns brown-grey.
- **A 1.** About 60°, the standing angle. Neither the top of the crust nor the thickness of a tender is fully shown.
- **Co 1.** The left tenders and the corn run off the frame edges.
- **B 0.** The black-and-white wave plate is the loudest thing in the photo and competes with the breading. A glass and a board intrude at the top. No crop removes the plate.
- **S 2, T 2.** The food itself looks crisp, hot, and real. This is the photo's strength.

Brief for the retake: plain matte plate (B), side window light at 45° (L, A). Three tenders, one broken open to show the meat.

### Mia's Vegan Cupcakes cover

Gates pass.

| L   | E   | C   | A   | Co  | B   | S   | T   | R   | G   | Total | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | ----- | ------- |
| 1   | 2   | 1   | 2   | 1   | 0   | 1   | 1   | 1   | 1   | 11    | RETAKE  |

- **L 1.** Even overhead room light. No shadows, so the domes look flat.
- **C 1.** Slightly cool. The red liners are the strongest colour in the frame.
- **A 2.** Overhead suits a tray of 24 identical items; the repetition is the picture.
- **Co 1.** The box edges cut the frame at uneven angles.
- **B 0.** The cardboard box fills the frame. A yellow package intrudes at the top right; a crop removes that, but not the box.
- **S 1.** Honest, but transport packaging rather than a serving.
- **T 1.** The sugar dusting reads. The crumb, which is what makes an egg-free cupcake interesting, is not visible.
- **G 1.** The box is slightly keystoned.

Brief for the retake: background (B) and texture (T). Three cupcakes on a plain plate at 20° to 45°, one unwrapped and broken open, window light from behind.

### Patatas y Batatas Bravas process photo

Graded as a process photo for method step 3. Gates pass: it shows the parboiled cut size, which is the cue for that step.

| L   | E   | C   | A   | Co  | B   | S   | T   | R   | G   | Total | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | ----- | ------- |
| 1   | 1   | 1   | 2   | 2   | 2   | 2   | 1   | 1   | 2   | 15    | EDIT    |

- **L 1, E 1.** Dim indoor light from above. The photo is a little dark.
- **C 1.** A mild cool cast.
- **A 2, Co 2, B 2.** Close and overhead; the cubes fill the frame and nothing else competes.
- **T 1.** The cut edges are visible but low in contrast.

Editing exposure and colour (✂ +1 each) would bring it to 17 with no 0. Edit it, then score again. If brightening makes it look noisy, retake it the next time the dish is cooked.
